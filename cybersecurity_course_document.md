### Concentration Tracks (Typical Options)

1. **Offensive Security** - Penetration testing, ethical hacking, red team operations
2. **Defensive Security** - Blue team operations, SOC analysis, incident response
3. **Governance and Compliance** - Risk management, policy development, audit
4. **Cloud and Virtualization Security** - AWS/Azure security, container security
5. **Digital Forensics and Incident Response** - Computer forensics, malware analysis
6. **Application Security** - Secure coding, application testing, DevSecOps

### Major Methods and Methodologies Taught

1. **Defense-in-Depth** - Layered security approach across network, host, application, and data levels
2. **Threat Modeling** - STRIDE, PASTA, attacker-centric risk assessment
3. **Security Development Lifecycle (SDL)** - Integrating security into SDLC
4. **Incident Response Framework** - NIST SP 800-61, SANS IR process
5. **Risk Management** - NIST RMF, ISO 27001, FAIR model
6. **Cryptographic Protocols** - TLS/SSL, PKI, digital signatures, hash functions
7. **Network Security Monitoring** - Log analysis, SIEM, packet inspection
8. **Vulnerability Assessment** - Scanning, penetration testing, CVSS scoring
9. **Compliance and Auditing** - Control frameworks, evidence collection, reporting
10. **Forensic Analysis** - Evidence preservation, chain of custody, analysis tools

### Program Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the 4-year program, graduates will be able to:

1. **Analyze** cybersecurity threats, vulnerabilities, and risks across various systems
2. **Design** and implement secure networks, systems, and applications
3. **Execute** incident response and forensic analysis procedures
4. **Apply** cryptographic techniques and understand their limitations
5. **Evaluate** compliance requirements and implement appropriate controls
6. **Develop** security policies and governance frameworks
7. **Communicate** effectively technical and non-technical stakeholders
8. **Engage** in continuous learning and professional development
9. **Utilize** industry-standard tools and methodologies
10. **Integrate** ethical considerations into security decision-making

### Typical Degree Requirements Summary

- **Total Credit Hours**: 120-124 credits
- **Core Mathematics**: Calculus I, Discrete Mathematics, Statistics
- **Core Computer Science**: Programming, Data Structures, Algorithms, Operating Systems
- **Cybersecurity Core**: 30-35 credits of specialized coursework
- **General Education**: 30-40 credits across humanities, social sciences, natural sciences
- **Electives**: 15-20 credits for concentrations or minors
- **Capstone/Senior Project**: 3-4 credits integrating comprehensive knowledge
- **Co-op/Internship**: Many programs require 1+ year of practical experience
